📖 HOW TO PLAY

  1. Every player has 4 tokens (2 in Quick mode).
  2. Roll a 6 to bring a token out of home.
  3. Tap a glowing token to move it. A 6 gives another roll.
  4. Landing on an opponent captures it (not on safe squares or stars) and gives a bonus roll.
  5. Three 6s in a row cancels your turn.
  6. You need the exact number to enter the centre. First to bring all tokens home wins.
